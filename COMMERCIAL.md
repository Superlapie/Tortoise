# Commercial licensing

Tortoise is **public source** and **free for noncommercial use**.

You may read the code, fork the repository, contribute pull requests, learn from it, and use it for personal, hobby, educational, and other noncommercial purposes under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## When you need a commercial license

You need a paid commercial license from Superlapie if you use Tortoise — including any part of its source, WPF app, CLI, broker, recovery tooling, or diagnostics pipeline — in connection with a **commercial purpose**.

That generally includes:

- A commercial product or service sold to customers or clients
- Managed IT, MSP, or endpoint-management offerings that use Tortoise for client machines
- Internal studio or enterprise tooling used to ship or support commercial products
- SaaS, hosted, or managed offerings built on or around Tortoise
- Client work where the deliverable is a commercial product or paid service
- Redistributing Tortoise, or a modified/forked version of it, as part of a commercial offering

## What stays free without a commercial license

Noncommercial use remains free, including:

- Personal learning and experimentation on your own machines
- Hobby projects with no commercial intent
- Contributing bug fixes, docs, and features back to this repository
- Forking for study, local experimentation, or noncommercial community work
- Use by qualifying noncommercial organizations under the PolyForm license terms

## What a commercial license grants

A commercial license is a separate written agreement that grants rights not included in the public noncommercial license, such as:

- Using Tortoise in a shipped commercial product or paid service
- Using Tortoise in an MSP or enterprise pipeline for client or fleet driver management
- Redistribution or embedding in a commercial toolchain, subject to agreed terms

Exact scope, pricing, support, and sublicensing terms are negotiated per customer.

## How to get a commercial license

Contact **Superlapie**:

- Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/Superlapie/Tortoise/discussions) with the category **Q&A** or **General**, or
- Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Superlapie/Tortoise/issues) titled `Commercial license inquiry`

Include:

1. Your name or company
2. What you want to build or ship
3. How Tortoise would be used (desktop app, CLI in CI, broker integration, forked codebase, etc.)
4. Expected team size and distribution model

I review commercial inquiries promptly.
