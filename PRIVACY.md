# Privacy Policy

Tortoise defaults to **local only** operation.

## What stays on your PC

- Device and driver inventory from scans
- Update plans and transaction history
- Local logs and diagnostic bundles you export
- Application settings

## What Tortoise does not collect by default

- Hardware IDs sent to a server
- Device serial numbers
- Machine or account names
- Installed device lists uploaded anywhere
- IP addresses, Windows product keys, BitLocker data
- Telemetry or analytics

## Accounts

Tortoise does not require an account.

## Diagnostic export

The diagnostic bundle exporter will show exactly what will be included and redact usernames, computer names, serial numbers, and tokens by default.

## Future crash reporting

If added, crash reporting will be explicitly opt-in and redacted.
